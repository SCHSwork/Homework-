The server monitor saves every check to the status-data branch of this repo, forever (raw checks for 7 days, daily summaries after that). This browser also keeps its own copy: the last 24 hours of checks in full, daily summaries forever, up to 1000 incidents and 500 benchmark runs.
Checks run in your browser from your network, so a "down" result can also mean your network blocks that host.
